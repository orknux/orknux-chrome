# Privacy policy: the Orknux extension for Chrome

The Orknux extension lets the agents of an Orknux server - one you sign in to
and choose - use this browser on your behalf: open pages, read them, click,
type, press keys, scroll and take screenshots.

**What it handles.** The address of your Orknux server and a credential for
it, which it keeps in Chrome's storage on this computer. While it is connected,
the content of the pages your agent works on - their text, the structure of
their controls, and screenshots of them - when your agent asks for it.

**Where it goes.** Only to the Orknux server you connected it to, and only the
pages and screenshots an agent of yours asked for, as the answer to that
request. The extension sends nothing to its authors or to anyone else, has no
analytics, and loads no code from anywhere.

**Whose pages.** Only tabs the agent opened itself, in a window of its own,
grouped as "Orknux". A tab you opened is refused: your own browsing is not read.

**Your control.** Closing the side panel, pressing Disconnect, or pressing
Cancel on Chrome's "is debugging this browser" bar stops it at once. Removing
the extension removes the address and credential it kept.

**What the server does with it** is the server's: an Orknux server is run by
you or your organisation, and keeps what its agents see as part of the
conversation they had with you.
